LONG TERM ROADMAP

From student to neurosurgeon

A U.S. oriented overview. Your exact path will depend on your future choices.
1

Now

Build science, math, reading, writing, and study habits. Explore anatomy and neuroscience at a fun, age appropriate level.

2

Middle & high school

Keep building biology, chemistry, physics, mathematics, communication, and research skills.

3

College

Complete an undergraduate education and prepare for medical school with science, communication, and service experiences.

4

Medical school

Study anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology, clinical skills, and the rest of medicine.

5

Neurosurgery residency

Neurosurgical residency in the U.S. is typically seven years, followed by continued specialization and learning for many surgeons.

6

Career long learning

Neurosurgeons continue training, studying research, reviewing cases, and learning new techniques throughout their careers.
